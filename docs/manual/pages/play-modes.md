## Campaigns

Nine campaigns tell the story of the war. All are open from the start. Pick one from **Campaign** on the title screen: it opens as a painted journey where each numbered stop is a chapter. Your heroes, their levels, skills and the equipment they wear carry over from chapter to chapter; each chapter raises the level cap and the creature tier you may build. A lost chapter can simply be played again. See [Campaigns](page:campaigns).

## New game

**New Game** first asks for a **mode**, then a map.

| Mode | Goal |
|---|---|
| Conquest | Be the last player or team standing. |
| Townless start | A lone hero, guarded villages to recruit from and a neutral town to take. |
| Survival | Hold your stronghold through a final siege. |
| Three strongholds | Keep three keeps in your hands. |
| Endless survival | Survival that never ends: each invasion is stronger than the last. |
| Expedition | Race to the centre of a labyrinth within 21 days. |

The map can be a **random map** (you choose its size, the number of players, its layout, teams, and how many monsters and how much treasure it holds; everything is rebuilt from the seed), one of the **curated seeds** (named maps that always play the same), or one of your own maps from the editor.

Random layouts include **Ring**, **Hub** and **Archipelago**, and for two players **Diagonal**, **Lanes** and **Branching**.

Each player slot is Human, Computer or Closed and picks a people. Several humans can share one screen; a curtain hides the map between turns. Players on the same **team** never fight, share their sight and win together, and can send each other resources from the town screen.

In a standard game each player starts with one hero, leading tier 1-2 creatures. The Frostmarch starts with two.

## Computer players

| Difficulty | How it plays |
|---|---|
| Passive | Builds and grows, but never goes after your heroes, towns or mines. |
| Easy | Fights only sure battles, keeps fewer heroes and earns a little less. |
| Normal | The standard opponent. |
| Hard | Takes riskier battles, hunts your towns and heroes, keeps more heroes and earns a quarter more. |
| Very hard | Weighs more battles, sees armies coming from further away, keeps up to five heroes and earns half again as much. |

The **Hard odds** setting hides every win chance; you judge fights from what your scouts tell you.

## Objectives

Scenario maps have orders, deadlines and side quests. The **Objectives** button lists them. Some missions have places to visit: a nest to seal, a watchtower to restore, a supply camp to burn, a relic to return. Quest relics travel in their own inventory, and adjacent heroes can hand them over.

## Saving

There are six save slots and an autosave, written at the start of every human turn. `F5` saves and `F9` loads. Campaign progress is saved on its own.

## The map editor

**Map Editor** on the title screen creates your own maps: terrain, roads, scenery, towns, mines, guards, treasure and heroes, with player slots and teams. Edited maps appear under Conquest in New Game.
