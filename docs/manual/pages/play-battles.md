## The forecast

When your hero moves onto an enemy, the game shows the **forecast** before anything is decided. It runs the battle two hundred times in its head and tells you:

- your chance of winning, as a word (*Overwhelming*, *Strong*, *Favorable*, *Even*, *Unfavorable*, *Severe*) and, if you have scouted the enemy, as a number;
- the losses to expect for each of your stacks.

From the forecast you can set your hero's **battle orders**, attack, send a scout, or withdraw. Tick *Watch the battle* to see it play out. Watching never changes the result.

## How a battle runs

A battle lasts up to fifteen rounds. Each round, every stack acts in order of **initiative** (with a little chance mixed in).

1. **Opening volley.** Before the first round every ranged stack fires once, both sides at the same moment.
2. **Approach.** Flyers reach the enemy in round 1. Other melee troops arrive in round 1 to 4, depending on their speed. Archers keep shooting all the while, so slow armies walk through arrows.
3. **The line.** While an army still has infantry or heavy troops standing, enemy melee can only reach its front and flanks. Archers and healers behind the line are safe until it breaks, or until enemy cavalry or flyers slip past it.
4. **The melee.** Stacks strike, the struck strike back, and the battle goes on until one side is gone or the rounds run out.

## The anvil and the hammer

Most battles are won the same way.

- **Pinning (the anvil).** The first melee stack to reach an enemy **pins** it. A pinned stack keeps fighting its pinner and cannot go after your archers.
- **Flanking (the hammer).** Cavalry and flyers that strike an enemy already pinned by your infantry get **no answer** from it, and its braced pikes do not stop them. Good cavalry waits a round or two for the line to hold before it charges.
- **Archers** prefer enemies that are pinned, and other archers.
- **Healers** stay behind the line, mend the most wounded stack and raise some of its fallen.

That is why a mixed army beats a pile of the biggest creatures you can afford: one huge stack has nobody to hold the line for it.

::: aside "Stack size"
Splitting a creature into many small stacks gains you nothing, and neither does one giant stack. A stack can only bring so many creatures to bear on one target; the rest wrap around to strike others. A volley that kills its target carries on into the next.
:::

## Damage, luck and morale

- **Attack against defense.** Each point of attack above the target's defense adds 3% damage (up to triple); each point below takes 2% away.
- **Fortune of war.** Each side rolls one luck multiplier per battle, up to 20% either way. A stronger army is a clear favourite, never a certainty.
- **Morale.** Each point gives a 4% chance of an extra action each round; negative morale risks a lost one. An army of one people gets +1 morale; mixing three or more peoples costs one. The dead of the Hollow Dominion have no morale at all.
- **Ground.** Every creature gets +1 attack on its people's native ground and -1 on hostile ground.

## Abilities

Creatures carry abilities: reach, flying, first strike, charge, poison, life drain, regeneration, fear and many more. Every people's page lists what its creatures can do, and hovering an ability in the game or in this manual explains it.

## Battle orders

A hero gives the army a **stance** and a **focus**. More options open as the hero levels up, and the Tactics skill opens them sooner. Your orders also hold when your hero is attacked.

## Battle arts and spells

Every hero class has a **battle art** that the hero uses on their own at the start of a round: a strike at the most dangerous enemy, a storm on every enemy stack, a healing light, a rallying cry, a hex or a ward. **Power** makes the art stronger and **Knowledge** lets the hero use it in more rounds. See [Heroes](page:heroes-guide).

## After the battle

The loser's army is destroyed. A defeated hero falls. The winner earns experience for everything it killed, and more for beating a hero.

## Veterans

Your stacks learn too. Every stack that fights beside a hero earns the hero's experience and rises through five ranks:

| Rank | Experience | What it brings |
|---|---:|---|
| 1 | 0 | The creature as recruited. |
| 2 | 1,500 | +1 attack and defense. |
| 3 | 5,000 | Becomes its upgraded form, with its abilities. |
| 4 | 13,000 | Another +1 attack and defense. |
| 5 | 35,000 | **Ascendant:** +20% health and damage, +2 attack and defense, and a form of its own. |

Upgraded creatures recruited in a town start at rank 3. Only stacks of the same creature at the same rank can merge. The Veteran Training skill speeds all of this up.
