The best place to start is **The Last Light**, the Concord campaign. Its first chapter, *Greyhold Harvest*, is a tutorial: the dead in the barrow fields will not come for you, so you can learn at your own pace. This page covers the same ground.

## The adventure map

You see the land from above. Your **hero** is the rider with your banner. Everything a hero can see is lit; the rest lies under a smoky shroud until someone scouts it.

1. **Click a tile** to plan a route. The path appears with the days it will take.
2. **Click it again** to march. `Space` also follows a planned route, and a right-click cancels it.
3. Each day a hero has a store of **movement**. Roads are cheap, forests, swamps and snow are dear. When the bar is empty, the hero waits for the next day.

Point at anything on the map to see what it is: a town, a mine, a pile of timber, a monster guarding a pass. Hostile armies show a rough size ("a pack of", "a horde of") and how a fight would likely go for the hero you have selected.

## Claim what you can

- **Resource piles** are picked up by walking onto them.
- **Mines** (a sawmill, an ore pit and the rarer mines) are claimed by walking onto them and pay you every day after.
- Some piles and mines are **guarded**. A pile next to a monster cannot be taken until the monster is beaten.

There are seven resources. **Gold** pays for almost everything. **Timber** and **ore** raise buildings. **Quicksilver, brimstone, crystal and gems** are the rare four; each people leans on two of them.

## Build and recruit

Open your town with `T`. Each town has a tree of buildings: a hall that raises your income, walls, a market, a tavern, and one **dwelling** per creature tier.

- You can raise **one building per town per day**, so choose. A dwelling now means troops this week; a better hall means more gold for every week after.
- Dwellings fill up every week. Recruit with the hero standing in the town, and the creatures join the hero's army. Without a hero in town they join the garrison.
- In a campaign, each chapter caps the tier you can build. The briefing tells you which tier is open.

## Fight

Walk onto an enemy to attack. Before anything happens, the **forecast** shows both armies, your chance of winning and the losses to expect. You can still turn back.

Battles fight themselves. You win them before they start: by bringing the right creatures, in the right numbers, to the right ground. See [Armies and battles](page:play-battles) for how that works.

## End the day

Press `E` to end the day. Your towns and mines pay out, your heroes get their movement back, and the other players move. Every seventh day a new week begins and the dwellings fill.

::: tip "Before you take the camp"
Pick up every pile within reach on day one, build a dwelling on day one, and attack the weakest guard first. Your hero gains experience from every victory, and experience brings levels and skills.
:::
