## What a hero does

Armies only move with a hero. A hero leads up to ten stacks, gives them their battle orders, adds their own attributes and skills to every fight, and gains experience from every victory. Lose a hero and you lose the army they led.

Each people has three **hero classes**, and two expansion peoples have three more each. Every class has its own **trait**, always on, and its own **battle art**. Campaign heroes are named characters with portraits of their own; tavern heroes are hired in towns.

## Attributes

| Attribute | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Attack** | Every creature in the army hits harder. |
| **Defense** | Every creature in the army takes less damage. |
| **Power** | Makes the battle art stronger. |
| **Knowledge** | Lets the hero use the battle art in more rounds, and holds more mana for spells. |

Each level adds one point to an attribute, following the class's leanings: a Knight grows mostly in attack, an Oracle in power.

## Levels and skills

Experience comes from battles, landmarks and some encounters. Levels come slowly: clearing a whole map is worth one or two levels for a new hero. At each level the hero is offered skills to learn or improve. Each skill has three levels.

Each class favours some skills over others, and some skills only one class or one people is ever offered. See [Skills and spells](page:heroes-skills) for the full list.

## The battle art

At the start of a round, the hero uses the class's battle art without being told to. Its strength grows with **Power** (and the Sorcery skill), and the hero can use it in one more round for every two points of **Knowledge**, up to five.

| Kind | What it does |
|---|---|
| Strike | Damages the most dangerous enemy stack. |
| Storm | Damages every enemy stack. |
| Mend | Heals the most wounded stack and raises some of its fallen. |
| Rally | Raises the army's morale for the round. |
| Hex | The enemy stack deals less damage for the round. |
| Ward | The army takes less damage for the round. |

## Spells

From level 2 a hero knows one spell that fits the class, cast on the adventure map with **mana**. Mana grows with Knowledge and refills a little each day. Rarer spells, such as returning home, travelling between your towns or a great burst of speed, are learned at shrines on the map.

## Equipment

A hero wears up to seven pieces: **weapon, helm, armour, shield, boots, charm and banner**, and carries eight more in the pack. Only what is worn counts. Equipment is found guarded on the map, bought from traders, won in encounters and given as campaign rewards. Some pieces belong to **sets** that give more when two or three are worn together. See [Equipment](page:heroes-equipment).

In a campaign, your heroes keep what they wear from one chapter to the next, along with a few pieces from the pack. Every other map starts empty-handed.
