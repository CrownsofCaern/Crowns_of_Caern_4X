## The lands

Caern is the world shared by the eight rival powers. Its known lands lie between the Ancient Glacier in the north and the sea that wraps around its east and south. Everything in it was shaped, one way or another, by the fire under the Wound.

{{world_map}}

- **The Frostmarch**, in the far north under the Ancient Glacier: snowfields, long halls and the clans who live in them.
- **The Sunken Forge**, the mountain spine in the west, hollowed by a thousand years of smith-clans into holds and galleries.
- **The Concord Marches**, in the centre, on the river roads: farmland, walled towns, slate-roofed citadels. Greyhold stands at their southern edge, Highmere is the fortress on the eastern road.
- **The Elderwold**, west of Greyhold: once the oldest forest in the world, now felled to stumps. Only the Last Grove still stands.
- **Sallowmere**, the southern fens: grave-towns, barrows and drowned chapels, ruled by the dead.
- **The Wound**, in the east: a ring of black rock around a burning chasm, with two passes in and out.

Under the land run **the Underways**. The Barrow Below is the dead's grave-realm. The Forge's galleries and the Host's tunnels lie beside it. Deeper than any of them are the caverns of the Umbral Court.

## Before the peoples had names

Long before any crown, the dark elves of the Umbral Court bound a dragon under the world. Its name was Karzhaloth. They cut out its six eyes and made them into the **Gate-Eyes**, the keys of the Nightgate that holds it. The Court gave one Eye to each of the surface peoples, so that no single hand could ever open the gate again, and withdrew below.

The surface forgot. The Eyes became crown jewels and relics: the jewel in a saint's reliquary, the keystone of a forge's arch, a regent's pendant.

## The Burnings

Every few centuries, the **Ashen Host** climbs out of the Wound. The demons are the fire the world was built on top of, and they want out. Each time they were driven back. None of those wars ever shut them in.

About four hundred years ago, in the **Second Burning**, the learned city of Sarranth on the coast fled into the sea behind a breathing-spell. A northern shaman named Skadric Whalebone changed one rune of it out of envy. Sarranth's people survived as the Shoal: fish-folk, strong, long-lived and slow of mind. The surface forgot them too.

## The Sealing

A hundred years ago, five peoples did what no one had done before. They shut the Host inside the Wound.

- The **Sunken Forge** cast the **Lockstone**, a seal of runed rock.
- The **Elderwold's Heartroots**, the deepest roots of the oldest trees, reached down and gripped it.
- The **Frostmarch Clans** marched their winter into the chasm to bank the fire.
- The **dead of Sallowmere** marched with the living.
- The **Aldmere Concord** held the gate while it was done. Sir Orrin Wren held it longest, and died there.

The Umbral Court was not asked, and did not come. Neither did the Shoal; no one remembered it was there.

## The debt

Forty years ago the Concord stopped paying the Forge for the Lockstone's upkeep. The Forge shut its gates. In the same years, the Concord began felling the Elderwold for its foundries. Nobody in the Concord asked what the roots were holding. Nobody in the Thornwood told them.

Thirty years ago, Concord soldiers burned a Thornwood village and carried off a girl named Branwen Ashbark. She grew up as Maelis Thorne, ranger captain of Greyhold.

## The Year of the Red Sky

This is the year the six main campaigns tell, each from one side.

The felled roots let go. The Lockstone cracks. The earth of the south grows warm, and the dead flee the heat, north onto the living's land. The Host breaks out of the Wound. Ash from the burning chasm turns the sky red, and in the north the summer never comes: the clans call it the **Ashwinter**.

No faction is stupid and no faction is right. Everyone in this war has a reason, and the six campaigns share their battles: the Burning Ford, the Iron Pass and Saint Orrin's Rest are fought from both banks.

## The Year After

The Host is broken but not gone. The surface peoples are exhausted, in each other's debt, and holding six old gems they do not understand. The two expansion campaigns, *The Six Eyes* and *The Drowned Oath*, and the short *Long Watch*, are set in this year.
