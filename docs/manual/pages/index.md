<div class="prose">

**Crowns of Caern** is a turn-based strategy game of heroes, armies and towns. You lead a hero across a painted adventure map, claim mines and treasure, build up your towns and raise creatures in them. When armies meet, the battle resolves on its own from what you brought: the size of your stacks, their abilities, the ground, your hero's skills. The war is won on the map, in the choices you make before the first blow.

This manual covers the world and its history, the eight peoples who fight over it, their heroes and creatures, every campaign, and the rules of play.
