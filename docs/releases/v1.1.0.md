**Crowns of Caern 1.1.0** brings a new soundtrack and a quality-of-life update: a clearer town and hero screen, an Options menu, more to do while waiting online, and spells to find on the map.
Crowns of Caern is an original dark-fantasy, turn-based strategy game made with Godot.

## New in 1.1.0

- **New music:** an original score for the title screen (*Embers of the Elder Vale*), the overworld (*Mosslight* and a second theme, with the title theme as a third) and battles (*Stone and Standard* and *Through the Ashen Pass*).
- **Online: look around while others move.** Instead of a black screen you see your own side (map, towns, heroes, armies) to read and plan. Orders wait for your turn.
- **Options menu** on the title screen and in the Esc menu: fullscreen (also F11), brightness and the sound settings.
- **Route cost:** a planned route you can reach today marks on the movement bar what it leaves.
- **Build reminder:** a hammer in the map's top-right corner while a town can still build today; click it to open that town.
- **Ten new spells and spells to find:** Stoneskin, Warcry, Unbroken Line, Hunter's Mark, Rootwalk, Red Harvest, Veil of Shadows, Dawnbreak, Call the Pack and Horn of the Hold join the rare spells (found at spell shrines, and now with a small chance in won encounters). Every spell has its own icon.
- **Town screen:** each dwelling lists only its best unit, with a small button to switch to the base unit. The market shows resource icons, and amounts snap to whole units of what you buy.
- **Hero screen:** a new Experience tab shows each stack's veteran level and progress, one stack at a time.
- **Battle forecast:** orders and formation are folded under *Advanced*. The 3D battlefield is now off by default (tick 3D to use it).
- Saves from 1.0.0 load unchanged.

## What's in the game

- **Eight playable factions** with their own creatures, building trees, heroes and faction mechanics: the Aldmere Concord, the Ashen Host, the Hollow Dominion, the Thornwood Covenant, the Sunken Forge, the Frostmarch Clans, the Umbral Court and the Abyssal Shoal.
- **Nine campaigns** told as painted regional journeys, including the three-chapter *The Long Watch*.
- **New games on handmade or generated maps:** random maps rebuilt from a seed (size, players, layout, teams, monsters and treasure), curated seeds and your own editor maps.
- **Game modes:** conquest, townless start, survival, three strongholds, endless survival and expedition.
- **Automatic combat** shaped by terrain, army composition and hero skills, shown as an animated battle.
- **Heroes** that gain experience, skills, specialties and artifacts across a campaign; **towns** with faction buildings, dwellings and garrisons; seven resources and mines.
- **Computer opponents** at several difficulties, hot-seat play and online play.
- **Map editor** for terrain, roads, scenery, objects, heroes and players.
- Six save slots plus an autosave; keyboard, mouse and touch controls.

The [game manual](https://github.com/CrownsofCaern/Crowns_of_Caern_4X/blob/main/docs/manual/README.md) covers the rules and controls.

## Known limitations

- The builds are not signed or notarized, so Windows and macOS warn on first start (see above).
- The Windows executable uses Godot's default icon.
- Some text is small on low-resolution screens.
