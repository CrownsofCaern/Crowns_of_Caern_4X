**Crowns of Caern 1.0.0** brings online play: several people can now play one game on their own computers.
Crowns of Caern is an original dark-fantasy, turn-based strategy game made with Godot.

## New in 1.0.0

- **Online play:** one computer hosts the game in progress and the others join with its address (over the internet with Tailscale or ZeroTier, no router settings needed). Whoever is to move plays the whole turn; End Turn hands the game on. Join a game at any time, take over a player whose person left, and resume a match from the Online save slot. See the [online guide](https://github.com/CrownsofCaern/Crowns_of_Caern_4X/blob/main/docs/ONLINE.md).
- **New adventure map cursors:** ten dark-fantasy cursors, including a capture flag for mines, towns and dwellings you can take.
- Saves from 0.1.0 still load (as offline games).

## What's in the game

- **Eight playable factions** with their own creatures, building trees, heroes and faction mechanics: the Aldmere Concord, the Ashen Host, the Hollow Dominion, the Thornwood Covenant, the Sunken Forge, the Frostmarch Clans, the Umbral Court and the Abyssal Shoal.
- **Nine campaigns** told as painted regional journeys, including the three-chapter *The Long Watch*.
- **New games on handmade or generated maps:** random maps rebuilt from a seed (size, players, layout, teams, monsters and treasure), curated seeds and your own editor maps.
- **Game modes:** conquest, townless start, survival, three strongholds, endless survival and expedition.
- **Automatic combat** shaped by terrain, army composition and hero skills, shown as an animated battle.
- **Heroes** that gain experience, skills, specialties and artifacts across a campaign; **towns** with faction buildings, dwellings and garrisons; seven resources and mines.
- **Computer opponents** at several difficulties, hot-seat play and online play.
- **Map editor** for terrain, roads, scenery, objects, heroes and players.
- Six save slots plus an autosave; keyboard, mouse and touch controls.

The [game manual](https://github.com/CrownsofCaern/Crowns_of_Caern_4X/blob/main/docs/manual/README.md) covers the rules and controls.

## Known limitations

- The builds are not signed or notarized, so Windows and macOS warn on first start (see above).
- The Windows executable uses Godot's default icon.
- Some text is small on low-resolution screens.
