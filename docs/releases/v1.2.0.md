**Crowns of Caern 1.2.0** makes the computer a real opponent and adds town sieges and an online lobby.
Crowns of Caern is an original dark-fantasy, turn-based strategy game made with Godot.

## New in 1.2.0

- **Tougher computer players:** every level above Easy moved up a step. Normal now plays like the old Hard, Hard like the old Very hard, and Very hard is new and merciless (175% income, closer gambles, up to six heroes). Easy is unchanged.
- **The computer goes to war:** from Normal up it scouts out where your towns and heroes are, fights its way through the guarded passes out of its own lands, and strikes when human players have worn each other down. Human players' towns and heroes are its favourite targets.
- **Town sieges:** a defended town holds out one day per fortification. The first attack starts the siege, the defenders may surrender early when badly outnumbered, and the siege lasts only while a besieging hero stays next to the town. The town view shows the days left and a new Treasury row with your resources.
- **Online lobby:** the host opens a lobby, and each player who joins picks a faction and hero class before the host starts the match. Changing the map or seed in game setup now keeps each slot's choices.
- **Battles:** figures in the 2D battle view keep their creature's size however many share a stack.
- **Map fixes:** after winning the battle for a guarded dwelling, its recruit window opens straight away (and a click on the hero's own tile opens it again). The hover card of a rare building says which of your heroes have already visited it.
- Saves from 1.1.0 load unchanged.

## What's in the game

- **Eight playable factions** with their own creatures, building trees, heroes and faction mechanics: the Aldmere Concord, the Ashen Host, the Hollow Dominion, the Thornwood Covenant, the Sunken Forge, the Frostmarch Clans, the Umbral Court and the Abyssal Shoal.
- **Nine campaigns** told as painted regional journeys, including the three-chapter *The Long Watch*.
- **New games on handmade or generated maps:** random maps rebuilt from a seed (size, players, layout, teams, monsters and treasure), curated seeds and your own editor maps.
- **Game modes:** conquest, townless start, survival, three strongholds, endless survival and expedition.
- **Automatic combat** shaped by terrain, army composition and hero skills, shown as an animated battle.
- **Heroes** that gain experience, skills, specialties and artifacts across a campaign; **towns** with faction buildings, dwellings and garrisons; seven resources and mines.
- **Computer opponents** at several difficulties, hot-seat play and online play.
- **Map editor** for terrain, roads, scenery, objects, heroes and players.
- Six save slots plus an autosave; keyboard, mouse and touch controls.

The [game manual](https://github.com/CrownsofCaern/Crowns_of_Caern_4X/blob/main/docs/manual/README.md) covers the rules and controls.

## Known limitations

- The builds are not signed or notarized, so Windows and macOS warn on first start (see above).
- The Windows executable uses Godot's default icon.
- Some text is small on low-resolution screens.
