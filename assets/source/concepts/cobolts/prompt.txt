Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: first exploration concept art board for Reignem, an original dark gothic strategy game. Landscape sheet.
Primary request: a coherent neutral non-playable trader people named COBOLTS, with five recruit tiers and three levels of trading post. Small wiry reptilian humanoids with narrow snouts, swept-back small horns, dull rust and slate scales, weathered merchant clothing, suspicious intelligent expressions; grim believable proportions, never cute. Their identity is travelling commerce, concealed military strength, salvage and cunning.
Composition: upper half five clearly separated full-body character concepts at equal presentation scale, left to right: T1 Pack Runner with hooked knife and laden pack; T2 Caravan Spear with spear and battered compact shield; T3 Bolt Broker with crossbow and bolt cases; T4 Cinder Alchemist with sealed ceramic fire pots and leather apron; T5 Vault Warden, a stout heavily armoured cobolt with broad shield and short war pick, still the same species. Lower half three clearly separated elevated three-quarter trading-post concepts left to right: L1 roadside canvas stall with one wagon; L2 fenced caravan yard with covered stalls; L3 fortified counting house with shuttered stone vault and canvas-covered rear yard. Closed awnings, screens and covered entrances conceal guards; no visible ranks of troops. Shared visual motifs: suspended balance scales, brass seals, layered patched canvas, iron-bound chests. Commercial wealth increases across levels.
Style: detailed hand-painted dark fantasy with slightly pixelated edges, strong dark brown outlines and readable silhouettes; muted charcoal, worn umber, tarnished brass, restrained petrol teal cloth and amber lanterns. Consistent top-left light, neutral smoky parchment backdrop, clearly spaced subjects, polished art direction exploration rather than final sprites.
Text: only small understated labels T1, T2, T3, T4, T5 below characters and L1, L2, L3 below posts. No other lettering.
Constraints: original designs, no recognizable franchise creatures, no dragons, no giant monster tier, no cute cartoon faces, no UI mockup, no animation sheet.
