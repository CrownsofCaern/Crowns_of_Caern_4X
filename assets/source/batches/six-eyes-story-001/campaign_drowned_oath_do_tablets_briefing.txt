Chapter illustration for Crowns of Cinder, campaign "The Drowned Oath" (Abyssal Shoal), chapter 1 "The Tablets of Sarranth". Approved dark-fantasy painted style of the existing chapter illustrations, 1536x1024. A drowned vault under a reef: coral-crusted pillars of an old human city, carved stone tablets half buried in sand under green water, a young fish-man (Ilo, slighter than the others, gilled, teal scales, pearl lamp) kneeling to read them while his huge friend Thunn, a shell-armoured tidemarshal with a coral trident, keeps watch at the stair. Above, through the water, a storm sky and the dark bulk of the reef citadel. No text, no HoMM references.
