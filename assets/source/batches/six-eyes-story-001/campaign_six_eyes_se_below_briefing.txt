Chapter illustration for Crowns of Cinder, campaign "The Six Eyes" (Umbral Court), chapter 1 "Below the Sky". Approved dark-fantasy painted style of the existing chapter illustrations, 1536x1024. The Umbral Court's cavern city: obsidian terraces and knife-like black spires lit by violet crystal lamps, hanging dark silk, a vast underground lake reflecting the lights. In the foreground Dreadmarshal Serane Nhalvor, an ash-grey dark elf in segmented obsidian armour, stands beside Matriarch Ulmarra, a hooded veilseer with a violet staff, both looking up at a stair cut into the far cavern wall that climbs toward a thin crack of grey daylight. House Draal's spears gather on a lower terrace. No text, no HoMM references.
