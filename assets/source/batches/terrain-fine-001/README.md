# Fine-scale terrain sources

Generated with ChatGPT image_gen for Reignem. Prompt: overhead dark-fantasy strategy map ground material, with small-scale details appropriate beneath buildings and heroes, subdued hand-painted texture, even lighting, no borders or large focal objects. Each source was prompted specifically for its terrain (forest needles and moss, gravel, grass, bog, snow, sand, cinders, highland stone, lake or river water). The detail variations add irregular small patches while keeping objects below roughly 1/60 of the source image.

The chosen sources are listed and hash-checked in `assets/specs/terrain.json`. Originals and alternate candidates are preserved here. They are source material, not directly loaded by Godot; `pipeline.py build` produces periodic fields and transition overlays.
